# Settings → Wallpaper

What must be visible when this tab is open:

- Tab header text: "Wallpaper" (i18n key `common.wallpaper`).
- Subtab bar with at least: "General", "Look", "Automation".
- An "Enable Wallpaper Management" toggle near the top.
- A wallpaper selector / open button.
- The "Look" subtab (the harness clicks it after opening): a "Fill mode"
  dropdown (`panels.wallpaper.look-feel-fill-mode-label`) offering the image
  scaling modes — Center / Crop / Fit / Stretch / Repeat
  (`wallpaper.fillMode`), a fill-color picker, and a transition-type
  dropdown with a transition-duration slider.
- The General subtab's "Position" dropdown selects where the wallpaper
  *selector panel* appears (`wallpaper.panelPosition`: follow-bar / center /
  top-center / corners) — it is NOT the image fill mode; do not confuse them.
- The standard left-side Settings tab strip is visible.
